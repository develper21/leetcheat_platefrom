## 🎯 Final Selection Criteria

### Must-Have Elements:
1. **Easy to Pronounce** (Global users)
2. **Memorable** (Word-of-mouth friendly)
3. **Available Domain** (.com, .in, .tech)
4. **Cultural Relevance** (Indian connection)
5. **Professional Sound** (Business trust)
6. **Scalable** (Can expand beyond coding)
7. **Unique** (No direct competitors)

### Nice-to-Have Elements:
1. **Short** (2-3 syllables ideal)
2. **Meaningful** (Deeper story)
3. **Visual Logo Potential** (Strong brand identity)
4. **Social Media Handles** (Available usernames)
5. **Trademark Clear** (No legal conflicts)
